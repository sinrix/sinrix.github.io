Prospective 2026 validation

Protocol registered 2026-10-04; certified 2026 outcomes are pending. Immutable forecast records lock the edition, input hash, implementation hashes, eligible chambers and competitive subset before November 3.

Certified regular-election seat winners, combined with pre-election verified holdovers. Chamber organization is a separate dated target. Upper chambers and verified holdovers are included. Officially nonpartisan organization is kept separate from underlying partisan alignment.

Scores compare the same eligible cases against no-change seats and an earlier-cycle empirical probability benchmark when compatible certified training records exist. Brier, log loss, seat error, interval coverage and reliability are reported for all eligible cases and the predefined competitive subset.

Report cycle-balanced scores and election-cycle clustered uncertainty when enough cycles exist. A single 2026 cycle cannot estimate cycle-level sampling uncertainty; do not substitute independent-chamber confidence intervals.

Full-model historical validation requirements

Reconstruct each vintage with dated district maps, candidates, holdovers, polling and source cutoffs. Fit national transfer, incumbency and shared-error components using earlier cycles only. Score later cycles once; no test-outcome selection or fitting. Existing structural and presidential residual studies do not satisfy this protocol.

The existing structural archive achieved only about one additional correct majority call relative to no change across 142 cases with seven control changes. Its small number of cycles and safe-chamber prevalence limit inference. Presidential residual diagnostics do not validate the full correlated chamber model.
