Coverage limitations & institutional assumptions
  • Special elections and subsequent vacancies are outside the regular-election baseline.
  • October 3 nationwide review: reported presidential baselines and current terms come from MultiState; Open States provides a roster cross-check. These compilations do not replace primary certification.
  • Holdovers follow the reported current-term roster with reconciled totals. Verification here means roster and seat-count checks; it does not certify every seat against a primary state notice.

  • District residual error is at least 10 points. Historical diagnostics are limited to selected contested single-member races; the full chamber model is not calibrated.
  • Parameters have not been historically calibrated. Monte Carlo precision does not measure model accuracy.
  • State-specific environment unavailable; zero-centered prior with wider uncertainty.
